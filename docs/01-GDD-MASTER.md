# Torque Atlas — Master GDD

## Genre
Open-world driving/racing/vehicle simulation.

## Core loop
Drive → discover → enter events → earn → buy → maintain → tune → improve → travel farther.

Parallel loops:
- driving mastery;
- ownership and maintenance;
- competition and reputation;
- sandbox experimentation.

## Open-world content
Large/small cities, villages, suburbs, highways, country roads, mountain roads, hairpins, tunnels, bridges, industry, ports, airports, forest/dirt roads, beaches, circuits, car parks and abandoned zones.

## Activities
Circuit, street, sprint, point-to-point, rally, rallycross, endurance, time attack, drag, drift, hill climb, off-road, cruise, road trip, free roam and crash-test sandbox.

## Dynamic world
Day/night, rain, fog, snow, wind, storms, road wetness, standing water, ice, temperature and optional seasons. Weather must directly affect tire, brake, visibility and surface behaviour.

## AI traffic
Profiles: cautious, normal, hurried, aggressive and inexperienced.
Agents vary by patience, awareness, aggression, skill, risk tolerance and lawfulness.
Traffic obeys rules, overtakes, queues, parks, reacts to crashes/emergency vehicles and can reroute.

## Ownership and maintenance
Persistent state: mileage, engine hours, oil, coolant, tires, brakes, suspension, battery, accident history and repair history.
Repair modes: full, mechanical-only, cosmetic-only and DIY.

## Tuning
Intake/exhaust, turbo/supercharger, ECU, internals, transmission, differential, brakes, suspension, alignment, tires, wheels, aero, weight reduction, roll cage and body parts.
Every performance modification changes simulation parameters.

## Progression
Licences, reputation, championships, money, garages, vehicles, sponsors, teams, challenges and achievements.
Free roam never requires career participation.

## Realism profiles
- Arcade: strong assists and simplified damage/maintenance.
- Sport: realistic but accessible.
- Simulation: minimal assists and full core physics.
- Hardcore Simulation: full fuel, tires, temperatures, damage, wear, manual systems and maintenance.

## Multiplayer
Online free roam, ranked/casual racing, private servers, friends, crews, car meets, drift/cruise servers, events and leaderboards.
MVP target: 8–16 players.
Long-term target: 32–64 active players per session, subject to performance validation.

## Economy
Income: races, championships, sponsors, challenges and vehicle sales.
Costs: cars, fuel/charging, parts, maintenance, repairs, insurance, tuning and garages.

## Business model
Premium game, map expansions, vehicle packs, specialist packs, cosmetic DLC and major free system updates.
No pay-to-win.

## Visual identity
Technical realism using topographic lines, engineering schematics, telemetry and structural overlays.

## Working title
**Torque Atlas**

Tagline direction:
**Real Roads. Real Physics. Real Consequences.**
